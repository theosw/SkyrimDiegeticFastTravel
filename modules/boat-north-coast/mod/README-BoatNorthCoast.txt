Diegetic Travel - Northern Coast Boat Test

Adds one physical-map dialogue option to the seven ordinary public CFTO Route
1 ferrymen. CFTO remains authoritative for its actors, live fare global, and
arrival markers. The Enthralled Ferryman, Windstad Manor, Frostflow Lighthouse,
and Icewater Jetty are intentionally outside this public-route candidate.

Requires the shared Diegetic Travel parchment runtime and CFTO. This package
contains no artwork or voice assets.
