Diegetic Travel - Northern Coast Boat Test

Adds one physical-map dialogue option to seven public CFTO Route 1 ferrymen and
the gated Windstad and Icewater/Volkihar private services. CFTO remains
authoritative for its actors, state globals, live fares, and arrival markers.
Frostflow Lighthouse is available from every active provider as a one-way
destination and never as a return source. Volkihar retains its 100-gold
outbound fare and free return.

Requires the shared Diegetic Travel parchment runtime and CFTO. This package
contains no artwork or voice assets.
