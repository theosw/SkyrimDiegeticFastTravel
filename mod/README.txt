Diegetic Travel — carriage alpha

Requirements:
- Skyrim Special Edition / Anniversary Edition
- SKSE64
- JContainers SE
- Carriage and Ferry Travel Overhaul (CFTO)

Install this folder as a mod and place DiegeticTravel.esp after CFTO.esp.

For this alpha, disable "Better Carriage Destinations - CFTO.esp". Its map
dialogue bypasses the CFTO destination topics that Diegetic Travel currently
uses for route availability, fare, and travel-time quotes.

This alpha replaces CFTO's carriage destination pricing and availability with
the authored road network. CFTO ferries are intentionally unchanged. Helgen and
Granite Hill are deferred until their custom travel handoff is implemented.
